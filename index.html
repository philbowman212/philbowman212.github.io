Intro

With 8+ years of Python and 7+ years of SQL under my belt, I specialize in turning complex data problems into clean, actionable tools.

Identifying manual, repetitive workflows and replacing them with automated solutions using Power Automate, Python scripts, and CLI tools. The goal is always the same: give people their time back.
